weather model

A machine learning model that forecasts today's weather over the whole Earth, once a day.

what
neural networks (U-Nets): 1 for cloud, 3 each for temperature and pressure
learned from
40 years of world weather (ERA5, 1979 to 2018); temperature and pressure then tuned on 30 Open-Meteo days
training
16 minutes for cloud, under 7 per network for temperature and pressure before tuning, on one RTX 3080
input
the past 30 days of 7 fields: cloud, temperature, rain, pressure, wind east, wind north, water vapour
output
today's mean cloud, temperature and pressure in each of 1,152 cells

satellite model

A machine learning model that corrects where SGP4, the standard orbit formula, puts an object days ahead.

what
a neural network, mean of 3 copies
learned from
28.6 million examples from published orbit elements, 2017 to 2025
training
13 to 27 minutes per copy, on one RTX 3080
input
45 numbers: an object's current orbit and how it drifted from SGP4 over the past 1, 3, 7 and 15 days
output
a correction to SGP4's position: height, along track, across
