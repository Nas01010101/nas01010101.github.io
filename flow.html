what am i seeing here

The clouds and rain on the planet are the Open-Meteo forecast for this hour, on a 7.5 degree grid, not this project's model. How much of each cell is cloudy is data; the edge pattern inside a cell is drawn.

The satellites are CelesTrak's published orbits, moved to this second in your browser.

pipeline

Each step shows the equation it runs. A narrower stream carries fewer values, and the label beside it is the array shape leaving that step. Under train, one lane per input is drawn as wide as its weight, and the lanes merge into the one value predicted for each cell.

weather forecast vs this week

Mean absolute error, weighted by area. Each day is predicted using only the days before it. Persistence repeats yesterday; trailing mean averages the earlier days.

satellite position vs this week

Each row moves elements published that many days ago to the epoch of today's elements and measures the distance, in km. The drift column subtracts an along-track correction fitted on yesterday's pair.

what "actual" means here

Weather: Open-Meteo's own model output for past hours, not station readings. Satellites: SGP4 run from the newest published elements, not a tracking measurement. Both carry their own error, which these scores do not include.
