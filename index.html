About

I am a third-year B.Sc. student in mathematics and computer science at the Université de Montréal. I do undergraduate research on theoretical machine learning for sequential decision-making, with a focus on stability and distribution shift in imitation learning, in the Department of Mathematics and Statistics.

I work where statistical guarantees meet practical systems: conformal prediction, calibration, distribution shift, and the reliability of learned models. I also work on remote sensing for satellite radar imagery.

Publications

  • A. El-Ghoudane. RFI Detection in Sentinel-1 SAR Imagery via Pseudo-Label Distillation and Multi-Resolution Ensemble. IEEE International Conference on Image Processing (ICIP), 2026. Accepted, sole author. ESA ClearSAR Track 1 Grand Challenge.

Selected projects

  • ClearSAR: pseudo-label distillation and a multi-resolution ensemble for radio-frequency interference detection in Sentinel-1 radar imagery. Code behind the ICIP 2026 paper.
  • warrant (research, code private): a calibrated trust layer for language-model agents. A conformal world model and an action firewall on one distribution-free coverage guarantee, served over MCP.
  • parsimony (research, code private): certified prompt compression that drops as much context as a distribution-free certificate allows, bounding the probability that the answer changes.
