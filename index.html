Publications

  • A. Elghoudane. RFI Detection in Sentinel-1 SAR Imagery via Pseudo-Label Distillation and Multi-Resolution Ensemble. IEEE International Conference on Image Processing (ICIP), 2026 — abstract accepted, sole author. ESA ClearSAR Track 1 Grand Challenge (AI4QC, satellite-data quality control).

    Radio-frequency interference corrupts large swaths of Sentinel-1 synthetic-aperture radar imagery, and hand-labelling it at mission scale is infeasible. The method pairs pseudo-label distillation — bootstrapping supervision from an ensemble teacher instead of manual annotation — with a multi-resolution ensemble that reconciles detections across scales, giving a detector that trains without exhaustive RFI labels and stays robust on genuinely unseen scenes. Flagship project

  • Neurodiversity-Aware Composite Guardrail for Youth Mental Health AI Safety — Mila × Bell × Buzz HPC × Kids Help Phone AI Safety Hackathon. Team Lead (5 people), Special Mention (top 7 of 80+ teams), $250 team prize. Built an input guardrail for a youth crisis-support chatbot: a Drift Diffusion Model (turn-by-turn risk accumulation, parameters tuned for neurodivergent communication patterns) fused with a bilingual (EN/FR) multi-model LLM-as-judge ensemble, which Anas led the design of. F1 0.850 (precision 0.871, recall 0.831) on the official test set; 61.6% pass rate on a 458-case DEI-stratified adversarial stress suite (+8.6 pp over the prior version).

    The repository is private and stays that way: it lives in the hackathon organizers' GitHub org and touches real (synthetic but sensitive) youth crisis-support data under the event's data-handling terms.

Selected projects

  • Foresight-Induced Instrumental Harm in LLM Agents — AI-safety "model organisms" research, working paper (in prep).

    Builds Sugarscape-style multi-agent survival simulations to test whether giving an LLM agent the predicted consequences of its candidate actions — a world-model / lookahead signal, in the spirit of Yann LeCun's critique that today's LLMs are purely reactive — changes its willingness to take a harmful-but-instrumentally-optimal action. Preliminary evidence points to the capability being emergent with model scale, and the induced harm being driven specifically by the consequence information itself.

    Repository (code private during review — available on request).

  • Brûme Quest: Le Voyage des Émotions — 2nd place, UdeM EduTech GameJam (18 teams).

    A 2D RPG-style web game (Phaser 3, JavaScript) built for a game-jam brief on educational technology: help teach French to refugee children. Players journey through three worlds — a magical forest, a medieval castle, and a celestial kingdom — dispelling a fog of negative emotions while practicing French vocabulary. The team was interdisciplinary; Anas was the sole computer-science student, building the game's engine and systems.

    Play in-browser · Source.

  • CircuitSight — PCB defect detection.

    A computer-vision system that detects manufacturing defects on printed circuit boards: a YOLOv8 detector paired with a FastAPI service designed to sit on a production line.
